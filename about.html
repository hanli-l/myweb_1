Our Story

Xiangteng Machinery was founded in 2005 as a comprehensive machinery enterprise. Headquartered in Foshan City, Guangdong Province, the company boasts modern production bases and a professional design team

Over the past fifteen years, we have consistently adhered to the business philosophy of "Quality as Foundation, Innovation as Core", and strived to provide customers with high-quality mechanical products and premium service experiences.

公司产品涵盖客厅、卧室、餐厅、书房等多个系列，风格多样，满足不同客户的需求。无论是现代简约、欧式古典还是中式传统，我们都能为您提供满意的解决方案。
